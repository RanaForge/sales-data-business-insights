199,815

All orders with a missing Coupon_Code have zero coupon discount.

Therefore, missing Coupon_Code is interpreted as:

"No Coupon Used"

These records should NOT be removed during data cleaning.

12. NEGATIVE TOTAL AMOUNT INVESTIGATION
---------------------------------------

There are 5 records with negative Total_Amount.

These records were investigated individually.

The negative values occur because:

Total_Amount =
Order_Value + Shipping_Cost - Coupon_Discount

In these records, the coupon discount is ₹100 while the
order value plus shipping cost is less than ₹100.

Four of the records have Delivered status and one has
Cancelled status.

Because there are only 5 such records out of 250,000
transactions, they are extremely rare.

Decision:

The records will be retained rather than deleted.

They will be documented as data-quality anomalies.


13. ORDER VALUE VALIDATION
--------------------------

The relationship:

Order_Value = Quantity × Unit_Price

was tested.

Maximum calculation difference:

5.82 × 10^-11

This is effectively zero and is attributed to floating-point
precision.

Conclusion:

Order_Value is mathematically derived from Quantity and
Unit_Price.


Important ML consideration:

Quantity and Unit_Price should NOT be used to predict
Order_Value because this would create data leakage/trivial
prediction.

Instead, the ML project will focus on predicting future sales
using historical/time-based information.


14. PRODUCT PRICE VALIDATION
----------------------------

The following relationships were tested:

Discount_Amount =
Original_Price × Discount_Percent / 100

Selling_Price =
Original_Price - Discount_Amount

Maximum discount calculation difference:
0.005

Maximum selling price calculation difference:
1.455 × 10^-11

These differences are negligible.

Conclusion:

Product pricing calculations are internally consistent.

16. GENERAL DATA QUALITY OBSERVATIONS
-------------------------------------

The categorical values were inspected for obvious spelling,
capitalization, and naming inconsistencies.

No major inconsistencies were found.

The dataset appears to have a highly structured/synthetic
distribution in several areas, such as product categories
and brand counts.

Therefore, business insights should be interpreted carefully
and should not automatically be assumed to represent actual
real-world Indian e-commerce behavior.
