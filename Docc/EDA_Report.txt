EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS (EDA) REPORT
Sales Data Analysis & Business Insights Project

PURPOSE
This report summarizes data understanding, cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory analysis, RFM customer segmentation, and validation for the Indian E-Commerce Sales Analytics dataset.

1. DATASET OVERVIEW
- Sales: 250,000 records and 21 original columns.
- Customers: 40,000 records and 15 columns.
- Products: 2,000 records and 12 columns.
- Sales.Customer_ID links to Customers.Customer_ID.
- Sales.Product_ID links to Products.Product_ID.
- Order_ID is unique for each sales record.
- Sales contains 39,914 unique purchasing customers. The customer master contains 40,000 customers, so 86 customers have no sales records.
- All sales customer and product IDs match their respective master datasets.
- No duplicate full rows were found in the original sales data.

2. DATA CLEANING AND VALIDATION
- Order_Date ranges from 2024-06-01 to 2026-06-30. Delivery_Date extends through 2026-07-07.
- Coupon_Code was missing in 199,815 records (79.93%); these were labeled "No Coupon".
- Review_Text was missing in 129,970 records; these were labeled "No Review".
- Rating remains missing in 129,970 records (about 51.99%) and was not filled with invented values.
- No duplicate rows or duplicate Order_ID values were found.
- Delivery duration ranged from 2 to 7 days, averaging about 4.50 days; no negative durations were found.
- Product pricing fields were internally consistent, and Order_Value matched Quantity multiplied by Unit_Price.
- Five records had negative Total_Amount because coupon discounts exceeded Order_Value plus Shipping_Cost. Four were Delivered and one was Cancelled. The four negative Delivered records total -₹51.41.
- Customer_Age in sales matched the supplied age in the customer master. DOB-based ages were one year higher for all 40,000 customers as of 2026-06-30, so the supplied age was retained.
- Customer master Total_Orders and Total_Spent differed from transaction-derived values for 28,523 customers. These master fields may reflect a different snapshot and were not overwritten.
- Customer registration dates ranged from 2023-06-01 to 2024-05-31, and no orders predated registration.

3. FEATURE ENGINEERING
Created features include calendar fields (year, month, quarter, weekday, weekend indicator, and order hour), Delivery_Days, Delivery_Speed_Category, Has_Coupon, Has_Review, Is_Returned, Is_Cancelled, Is_Delivered, customer attributes and tenure, joined product attributes, and Product_Price_Category:
- Budget: selling price <= ₹5,000
- Mid-Range: selling price > ₹5,000 and <= ₹20,000
- Premium: selling price > ₹20,000

Daily time-series features include 1-day, 7-day, and 30-day revenue lags and 7-day and 30-day rolling means. Rolling calculations were shifted to avoid using current-day information in prior-period features.
The enriched sales dataset contains 250,000 rows and 50 columns. The daily time series contains 760 consecutive dates from 2024-06-01 through 2026-06-30.

4. ORDER STATUS AND RECORDED AMOUNTS
- Total orders: 250,000
- Delivered: 200,139 (80.06%)
- Cancelled: 12,507 (about 5.00%)
- Returned: 12,493 (about 5.00%)
- Shipped: 12,459 (about 4.98%)
- Processing: 12,402 (about 4.96%)
- All-status Total_Amount sum: ₹5,930,693,254.30 (about ₹593.07 crore)
- Delivered Total_Amount sum: ₹4,741,566,354.41 (about ₹474.16 crore)

These are sums of the dataset's Total_Amount field across the specified statuses. They should be described as recorded amounts, not automatically as recognized revenue, retained sales, or profit. Returned-order amounts may not represent refunds.

5. MONTHLY SALES TRENDS
Monthly recorded amounts were generally stable, commonly around ₹2.3–₹2.48 crore, with order volumes often around 9,800–10,400 per month. February 2025 and February 2026 showed dips; shorter month length may partly explain them. June 2026 is a full month in the dataset. The initial trend analysis included all order statuses, so it represents recorded amounts across statuses rather than delivered-only revenue.

6. PRODUCT CATEGORY PERFORMANCE (DELIVERED ORDERS)
- Electronics: 53,576 orders; about ₹344.33 crore; 72.62% of delivered recorded amount; average amount per order about ₹64,269.
- Home: 26,877 orders; about ₹57.33 crore; 12.09%.
- Sports: 23,410 orders; about ₹37.94 crore; 8.00%.
- Fashion: 39,842 orders; about ₹26.66 crore; 5.62%.
- Grocery: 26,778 orders; about ₹3.35 crore; 0.71%.
- Beauty: 13,427 orders; about ₹3.07 crore; 0.65%.
- Books: 16,229 orders; about ₹1.49 crore; 0.31%.

Electronics dominates delivered recorded amount, while Fashion has the highest delivered order count among these categories. Amounts do not represent profit because cost and margin data are unavailable.

7. PAYMENT METHOD ANALYSIS (ALL STATUSES)
- UPI: 128,474 orders (51.39%); about ₹317.64 crore recorded amount; average about ₹24,724 per order.
- COD: 82,093 orders (32.84%); about ₹184.19 crore; average about ₹22,437.
- Debit Card: 28,856 orders (11.54%); about ₹63.17 crore; average about ₹21,892.
- Credit Card: 10,577 orders (4.23%); about ₹28.07 crore; average about ₹26,535.

UPI had the highest order volume. Credit Card had the highest average recorded amount per order. Cancellation and return rates by payment mode were broadly similar, about 4.9%–5.1%; no causal conclusion was drawn.

8. GEOGRAPHIC PERFORMANCE (DELIVERED ORDERS)
Top states by delivered recorded amount:
- UP: about ₹61.87 crore (13.05%)
- Rajasthan: about ₹60.47 crore (12.75%)
- Haryana: about ₹59.77 crore (12.61%)
- Maharashtra: about ₹48.48 crore (10.22%)
- Punjab: about ₹47.68 crore (10.06%)
- Gujarat: about ₹47.60 crore (10.04%)
- Delhi: about ₹38.17 crore (8.05%)
- Tamil Nadu: about ₹37.60 crore (7.93%)
- West Bengal: about ₹36.47 crore (7.69%)
- Karnataka: about ₹36.04 crore (7.60%)

Delhi had the highest average recorded amount per delivered order among the top 10 states, at about ₹24,443. Average delivery duration was similar across states (about 4.49–4.51 days). Slow-delivery shares were about 32.9%–33.8%, showing no clear state-level outlier in this analysis.

9. CANCELLATION AND RETURN TRENDS
Monthly cancellation and return rates were generally around 4.6%–5.3%. Rates were somewhat higher in May and June 2026 than in several earlier months, but no cause was established. Continue monitoring before making operational decisions.

10. CUSTOMER TIER ANALYSIS (DELIVERED ORDERS)
- Platinum: 27,349 customers; 153,476 delivered orders; about ₹445.54 crore; 93.96% of delivered recorded amount; average 5.61 delivered orders per customer; about ₹162,908 per customer.
- Gold: 6,692 customers; 28,674 delivered orders; about ₹23.24 crore; 4.90%; average 4.28 orders per customer; about ₹34,733 per customer.
- Silver: 5,685 customers; 17,989 delivered orders; about ₹5.38 crore; 1.13%; average 3.16 orders per customer; about ₹9,458 per customer.

Platinum customers account for most delivered recorded amount. Tier assignment rules were not provided, so this report does not assume spending alone determines tier.

11. COUPON AND DISCOUNT ANALYSIS
For delivered orders:
- No coupon: 159,983 orders (79.93%); average recorded amount per order about ₹23,816.
- Coupon used: 40,156 orders (20.07%); average recorded amount per order about ₹23,194.
- Coupon discounts on coupon orders totaled about ₹50,056,422.55 (₹5.01 crore).
- Average coupon discount among coupon orders was about ₹1,246.55.

Coupon orders had an observed average recorded amount about ₹623 lower than orders without coupons, but this does not establish that coupons caused lower order values. Coupon_Discount is a monetary amount, separate from the product-level Discount_Percent field. Product discount percentage averaged about 27.47%, with a range of 5%–50%.

12. RFM CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION
RFM segmentation used Delivered orders only, with 2026-07-01 as the reference date. The RFM dataset contains 39,726 customers.
K-Means was evaluated for K=2 through K=8. K=2 had the highest sampled silhouette score (about 0.337); K=3 was selected for business exploration after considering the elbow pattern and interpretability. The three-cluster choice is exploratory, not proven statistically optimal.

Provisional segments:
- At-Risk Low-Spending: 10,154 customers (25.56%); average recency 246.38 days; average frequency 2.76 orders; average monetary value about ₹30,174.
- Recent High-Engagement: 10,534 customers (26.52%); average recency 24.19 days; average frequency 6.25 orders; average monetary value about ₹146,931.
- Established High-Spending: 19,038 customers (47.92%); average recency 163.33 days; average frequency 5.59 orders; average monetary value about ₹151,666.

Segment labels are provisional descriptions; K-Means cluster IDs have no inherent business meaning. Monetary value is based on Total_Amount for delivered orders.

13. FINAL RECONCILIATION AND QUALITY REVIEW
All checks below passed:
- 250,000 sales records and 250,000 unique orders; one row per order.
- Status counts reconcile to total orders.
- 39,914 unique purchasing customers and 2,000 unique products.
- 200,139 delivered orders.
- Delivered recorded amount: ₹4,741,566,354.41.
- All-status recorded amount: ₹5,930,693,254.30.
- No missing values in key fields: Order_ID, Customer_ID, Product_ID, Order_Date, Order_Status, Category, and Total_Amount.
- Category amounts reconcile exactly to the overall delivered recorded amount; difference ₹0.00 and rounded category shares total 100%.
- Daily time series has 760 continuous dates from 2024-06-01 to 2026-06-30.
- Expected missing time-series values: Revenue_Lag_1 = 1, Revenue_Lag_7 = 7, Revenue_Lag_30 = 30, Revenue_Rolling_Mean_7 = 7, Revenue_Rolling_Mean_30 = 30.
- Four delivered orders have negative Total_Amount values totaling -₹51.41. These remain documented anomalies.

14. BUSINESS OBSERVATIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Electronics accounts for 72.62% of delivered recorded amount. Examine category margins and concentration before strategic decisions.
2. Fashion has a high delivered order count but a much smaller amount share. Explore product mix, pricing, bundles, and cross-selling.
3. UPI is the most-used payment method. Maintain a reliable UPI checkout while supporting other methods.
4. Platinum customers contribute 93.96% of delivered recorded amount. Validate tier definitions and customer concentration; consider retention programs for high-value customers and engagement campaigns for other segments.
5. Monitor cancellation and return rates, including the higher observations in May and June 2026.
6. Test coupon effectiveness with a controlled analysis before expanding or reducing discounts.
7. Current analysis does not identify a strong geographic delivery-performance outlier.
8. Investigate negative Total_Amount records and confirm definitions of Total_Amount, returns, and refunds before financial reporting or modeling.

15. LIMITATIONS
- Definitions for Total_Amount, recognized revenue, refunds, and profit are not fully documented.
- Cost and margin data are unavailable, so profitability cannot be calculated.
- Customer tier assignment rules are unknown.
- Coupon comparisons are descriptive, not causal.
- Differences in cancellation and return rates have not been tested for statistical significance.
- RFM clustering is exploratory; validate cluster usefulness before using segments operationally.
- Missing ratings were retained because no valid replacement values were available.
- Customer master summary fields differ from transaction-derived totals for many customers and may represent a different snapshot.

16. CONCLUSION AND NEXT STEPS
EDA is complete for the current project scope. Dataset relationships, quality issues, major patterns, customer segments, and reconciliation checks have been documented.
Main findings include Electronics' dominance in delivered recorded amount, the high recorded contribution of Platinum-tier customers, UPI's leading order share, broadly stable monthly sales, and similar delivery times across states.

Next steps:
1. Create a clearly defined business KPI summary.
2. Build a sales forecasting model using a chronological train/test split, leakage-safe features, baseline comparison, and MAE/RMSE evaluation.
3. Integrate KPIs, charts, customer segments, and forecasting results into a Streamlit dashboard.
